# Academic Prose Revision

Improve the prose while preserving the author's intellectual texture. Optimize for a careful reader, not an AI-writing detector.

## Establish the contract

Before editing, identify the passage's claims, evidence, qualifications, terminology, placeholders, and collaboration conventions. Preserve them unless the author asks for a substantive change. When an author sample is available, read enough continuous prose to infer its rhythm and level of technical detail; do not copy its paper structure or stock phrases.

Read [voice-profile.md](references/voice-profile.md) when matching an author's style or deciding what “natural academic prose” means. Read [revision-procedure.md](references/revision-procedure.md) for a full concision, coherence, and flow pass.

Before presenting any revision, always complete the mandatory style review described in [revision-procedure.md](references/revision-procedure.md). This applies even when the requested edit is local: check the local unit's global coherence, topic-to-stress flow, paragraph bridges, and restrained elegance before handing it back.

## Revise with restraint

- Diagnose argument order before sentence style. A polished sentence cannot repair a paragraph whose claims arrive in the wrong order.
- Preserve useful explanation, mechanisms, examples, and qualifications. Concision means removing material that does no work, not minimizing word count.
- Prefer direct subjects and verbs, but retain passive voice when the object, procedure, or evidence should remain the grammatical focus.
- Keep adverbs, three-part lists, discourse markers, and long sentences when they carry real distinctions or rhythm. No construction is banned by form alone.
- Avoid generic openings, inflated contribution language, canned contrasts, repetitive sentence frames, and conclusions that merely announce importance.
- Consider several substantially different phrasings internally for an important paragraph, then choose the version with the best logic, precision, and voice. Do not present variants unless the author requests them.
- Stop when another pass would mainly exchange one acceptable sentence for another. Repeated rewriting often removes voice before it adds clarity.

## Deliver a reviewable revision

Follow repository editing rules, including visible macros when required. Summarize material changes to argument, emphasis, or section role. Do not describe minor synonym substitutions as substantive improvements.
