\subsection{Phrases}

We assemble $1{,}164$ fixed multi-word expressions of three kinds
(Table~\ref{tab:dataset}). \textbf{Idioms} ($852$) are drawn from the same four
sources used to build IDIOMEM \citep{haviv-etal-2023-understanding}:
MAGPIE \citep{haagsma-etal-2020-magpie} ($511$),
LIdioms \citep{moussallem-etal-2018-lidioms} ($165$),
EPIE \citep{saxena-paul-2020-epie} ($81$), and a public list of English idioms
and proverbs ($95$).\footnote{\url{https://www.ef.com/wwen/english-resources/english-idioms/},
the source \citet{haviv-etal-2023-understanding} label \texttt{EF}.} Our set is
not IDIOMEM itself: we retain items their predictability and prompt-similarity
filters remove, since our success criterion is a multi-token string match rather
than a single-token prediction. \textbf{Landmarks} ($127$) are the
multi-word entries of a public ``150 most famous landmarks in the world'' list,
and \textbf{film titles} ($185$) are the multi-word entries of the IMDb Top 250.
Neither is a curated research resource; both are convenience samples of
well-known proper names.

The three kinds are chosen to vary the reason a continuation is predictable.
An idiom is fixed by convention, a landmark name by reference, and a film title
by citation, but all three should be frequent in pretraining text, which is
what makes them a favourable test case. Phrases too short to admit a lookahead
position of at least $2$ are excluded.

\subsection{Natural contexts}

A phrase on its own is an odd thing to show a model: nothing tells it that a
set phrase is what it is looking at. So we also collect real occurrences.
Streaming $6.3$ million documents from
FineWeb-Edu \citep{penedo-etal-2024-fineweb}, we keep up to ten sentences per
phrase, each with up to $150$ tokens of preceding text. This gives two parallel views of the same expressions:
\textit{Empire \underline{\hspace{1.2em}}} on its own, and \textit{he went to
NYC to see the Empire \underline{\hspace{1.2em}}}. For phrases that are predominantly capitalised
(most landmarks and titles), we require the occurrence to preserve at least
$70\%$
of the original capitalisation, which prevents a sentence about sunrise from
being collected as an instance of the film \textit{Before Sunrise}.

This yields $9{,}955$ (phrase, context) instances covering $1{,}096$ of the
$1{,}164$ phrases, of which $925$ reach the full ten slots. Because the collector resumed
from an earlier five-context run and re-added what it already held, $44.8\%$ of
these are repeats, leaving $5{,}498$ distinct pairs. Decoding is greedy, so a
repeat gives the same outcome: overall rates barely move ($64.8\%$ versus
$64.5\%$ at $i=3$), but the effective sample is about half the instance count.
We report instance-level rates over the full set, to match the per-layer and
probing analyses that were run on it, and compute every per-phrase statistic
(\S\ref{sec:stability}) over distinct contexts only. Each instance gives one
trial per usable lookahead distance: $27{,}098$ in all, $24{,}740$ of them at
$i \in \{2,3,4,5\}$, with $9{,}148$ at $i=2$ falling to $2{,}386$ at $i=5$ as
longer lookaheads demand longer phrases.

Idioms dominate both the phrase set and the instance set ($20{,}371$ of
$24{,}740$ pooled trials), so aggregate rates run close to idiom rates; we
report per-category results separately in \S\ref{sec:disagreement}.

\subsection{Models}

Our main model is OLMo-2-7B \citep{olmo-2025-olmo2} in \texttt{bfloat16}, a
$32$-layer decoder-only model chosen because its training data is public, which
makes the assumption that these phrases were seen in pretraining checkable in
principle. We replicate the central measurements on OLMo-3-7B and, for
generation, on Qwen2.5-14B \citep{qwen-2024-qwen25} (Appendix~\ref{app:details}).

Our primary Patchscopes readout takes the union over eight layers,
$\{5, 7, 10, 13, 15, 20, 25, 30\}$. This is the layer set on which the
per-layer, per-category and probing analyses were run; storing hidden states
for all $32$ layers at every trial was not feasible, so using it throughout
keeps those results mutually comparable. Where a wider sweep is available we
report it alongside: \S\ref{sec:disagreement} quantifies what the budget costs,
which is six to eight points of Patchscopes recovery, and we state which layer
set every number reflects.
