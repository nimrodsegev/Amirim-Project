\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\input{tables/dataset}

We describe the phrases we test, the sentences we test them in, and the models
we run them on.

\subsection{Phrases}

We assemble $1{,}164$ fixed multi-word expressions of three kinds
(Table~\ref{tab:dataset}): \textbf{idioms} ($852$), fixed by convention;
\textbf{landmarks} ($127$), fixed by reference; and \textbf{film titles}
($185$), fixed by citation. The idioms come from the four sources used to build
IDIOMEM \citep{haviv-etal-2023-understanding}: MAGPIE
\citep{haagsma-etal-2020-magpie}, LIdioms \citep{moussallem-etal-2018-lidioms},
EPIE \citep{saxena-paul-2020-epie}, and a public list of English idioms and
proverbs.\footnote{\url{https://www.ef.com/wwen/english-resources/english-idioms/},
the source \citet{haviv-etal-2023-understanding} label \texttt{EF}. Our set is
not IDIOMEM itself: we keep items their predictability and prompt-similarity
filters remove, since our success criterion is a multi-token string match rather
than a single-token prediction.} Landmarks and film titles are the multi-word
entries of a public ``150 most famous landmarks in the world'' list and of the
IMDb Top 250; neither is a curated research resource. Phrases too short to admit
a lookahead position of at least $2$ are excluded.

\subsection{Natural contexts}

We also collect real occurrences, since a phrase shown on its own gives the
model no sign that a set phrase is what it is reading. Streaming $6.3$ million
documents from FineWeb-Edu \citep{penedo-etal-2024-fineweb}, we keep up to ten
sentences per phrase, each with up to $150$ tokens of preceding
text.\footnote{For phrases that are predominantly capitalised (most landmarks
and titles), we require the occurrence to preserve at least $70\%$ of the
original capitalisation, which prevents a sentence about sunrise from being
collected as an instance of the film \textit{Before Sunrise}.} Repeated
occurrences of the same context are collapsed, giving $5{,}498$ distinct
(phrase, context) pairs over $1{,}096$ phrases; most phrases contribute five and
only $12$ reach the full ten. Each pair gives one trial per usable lookahead
distance, $13{,}613$ in all at $i \in \{2,3,4,5\}$. Idioms dominate these trials
($11{,}104$ of them), so aggregate rates run close to idiom rates, and we report
per-category results separately in \S\ref{sec:disagreement}.

\subsection{Models}

Our main model is OLMo-2-7B \citep{olmo-2025-olmo2} in \texttt{bfloat16}, a
$32$-layer decoder-only model, chosen because its training data is public. We
replicate the central measurements on OLMo-3-7B and, for generation, on
Qwen2.5-14B \citep{qwen-2024-qwen25} (Appendix~\ref{app:details}). All three are
base checkpoints. Our primary Patchscopes measurement takes the union over eight
layers, $\{5, 7, 10, 13, 15, 20, 25, 30\}$, the layer set on which the
per-layer, per-category and probing analyses were run; \S\ref{sec:disagreement}
reports what that budget costs against a full sweep.
